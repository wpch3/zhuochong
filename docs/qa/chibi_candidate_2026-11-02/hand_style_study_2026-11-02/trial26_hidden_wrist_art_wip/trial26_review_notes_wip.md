# Trial26 hidden-wrist art edit review (WIP; rejected)

## Scope

Two independent AI edits were made from the trial24 @2× hand parts to test whether the approved basic mitten form could support a longer hidden wrist. The requested change was only an upward wrist continuation; the outputs were never treated as production parts.

The requested transparent background was again rendered as an opaque checkerboard. The artwork also deviates from the reference in scale and line/shadow treatment: viewer-left has a very broad, elongated tube-like wrist; viewer-right has an overly tall narrow shaft and a hard horizontal wrist/palm seam. This is not suitable for transparent parts and is not a source-aligned hand revision.

## Decision / boundary

Reject as part art. Keep both images only as negative WIP evidence. No matte removal, chroma-key, alpha reconstruction, cuff fitting, or C-rig insertion was attempted. Trial24 remains the provisional basic-shape base; its hidden-wrist art gap remains open. No production assets were changed.
